- **Analytics & BI Engineering** — semantic layer, data modeling, metrics and dashboards.
- **Data Team Advisory** — how data teams should be structured, scaled and aligned with business needs: roles, collaboration patterns, ownership models, data maturity.
- **Team Training** — practical workshops and knowledge-sharing sessions on modern data engineering, cloud platforms, BI and AI workflows.
